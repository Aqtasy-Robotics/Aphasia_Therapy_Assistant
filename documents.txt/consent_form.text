INFORMED CONSENT FOR CLINICAL TESTING
Project: Aqtasy Robotics Aphasia Therapy Assistant
Location: The Words Speech Therapy Clinic

1. Purpose of the Test
You are invited to participate in a clinical test conducted by a university research team. We are testing a conversational robot designed to help patients with Aphasia practice speech therapy. 

2. What You Will Be Asked to Do
- Duration: Testing takes place over 3 separate days.
- Time: Each session will last up to 2 hours.
- Activity: Purely conversational and interactive speech exercises. There is no physical contact or risk involved.

3. Data Collection and Privacy
We will fully transparently collect: audio recordings, text transcripts, accuracy scores, and basic demographic info. 
How we protect you: All data is fully anonymized. Your name will not be attached to the files in our database. Once evaluation is complete, all audio and transcripts will be permanently deleted.

4. Voluntary Participation
Participation is entirely voluntary and unpaid. You have the right to stop the session, take a break, or completely withdraw at any time, for any reason, without penalty to your standard care.
